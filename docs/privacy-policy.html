Usync Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

Usync ("Usync", "the app", "we") is an app for two people to stay emotionally connected: a shared drawing canvas, touch sync, mood sharing, a daily question, a shared bucket list, memories and a timeline. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. Usync is provided by [DEVELOPER / COMPANY NAME].

1. Information we collect

InformationWhat it is and why we need it
Google account detailsWhen you sign in with Google we receive your email address, your Google display name and an account identifier. We use them to create and recognise your account. We never see your Google password.
ProfileYour display name (taken from Google, and editable) and an avatar emoji. Your partner sees these.
PairingThe invite code you generate or enter, and the link between you and your partner. Invite codes expire after 24 hours.
MoodsThe moods you send (including custom text and emoji) and when you sent them. The mood itself is end-to-end encrypted (see section 7); we can see only that a mood was sent, by whom and when.
Canvas and memoriesDrawings you make on the shared canvas, and the memories you save, including their images, titles and dates. Images are stored in a private storage area.
Touch and presenceLive touch events and whether you are online are sent between the two of you in real time. They are not kept after the moment passes.
Location (optional)If you turn on location sharing, your last known location is stored and shown to your partner. It is updated when you open the app, and in the background only if you allow background location and your partner asks for it; you are notified each time. You control this in the app and in your phone's settings.
NotificationsThe alerts you and your partner send each other. The text of an alert that is sent to your phone's lock screen is a fixed sentence such as "Alex shared a mood 💭"; it never contains what you typed, but it does show the kind of activity (for example a mood, a bucket-list item or a touch) and who did it. The details are stored end-to-end encrypted and shown only inside the app. We keep only the 10 most recent per person. We also store a push token for each of your devices so we can deliver them, and your choices about which alerts are silent.
Daily questionYour answers to the question of the day (up to 500 characters each) and the day you gave them. Answers are end-to-end encrypted; we cannot read them. Your partner's answer is only shown to you after you have answered, and you only ever see your own answer before that.
Bucket listThe items you and your partner add to your shared bucket list (title, optional note, emoji) are end-to-end encrypted; we cannot read them. We can see who added an item, when, and when an item was ticked off and by whom.
Relationship type and next meetingWhether you chose "Together" or "Long-distance" for your couple, and, if you set one, the date you plan to meet next. Both are shared with your partner. The date is end-to-end encrypted; the relationship type is not.
Encryption keysTo protect your data with end-to-end encryption we store your public encryption key (your partner can see it), a recovery backup of your key that is sealed with a phrase only you know (we cannot open it), and your couple's key sealed separately for each of you (we cannot open it). We never receive your private key, your recovery phrase or your couple's key. If you lose your phone and your recovery phrase, nobody, including us, can recover your encrypted history.
StreaksThe days on which both of you did something in the app, used to show your day streak and moment count.
On your device onlyYour theme, notification and other settings, and a cached copy of your recent data so the app works offline. Biometric lock is handled entirely by your phone's operating system; we never receive or store your fingerprint or face data.

We do not use advertising, we do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we do not collect your contacts, photos library or microphone.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To connect you with your partner and run the features you use: canvas, touch sync, moods, the daily question, the bucket list, memories, timeline, streaks and location sharing.
  • To send you push notifications when your partner does something.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to prevent abuse.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Who can see your information

  • Your partner sees your profile, moods, canvas, memories, timeline, streaks, online status and, if you share it, your location. Nobody else in the app can see this. Access is limited to the two members of a couple by database access rules.
  • Service providers that process data for us (below). They may only use it to provide their service to us.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect people's safety.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications to alert you when your partner sends something.
  • Location (precise, approximate, and optionally background) only for location sharing. You can use Usync without it.
  • Biometrics / screen lock to protect the app. The app lock is on by default and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • Internet to talk to our servers.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • Disconnecting from your partner (Settings → Disconnect Couple) permanently deletes everything you shared: moods, memories and their images, timeline, canvas drawings, notifications, locations, daily-question answers, bucket-list items, your next-meeting date, your couple's sealed encryption keys and streak data. It cannot be undone.
  • Signing out removes your location from the server and your push token for that device, and clears the app's data on that device.
  • Deleting your account is described in section 6a below.
  • Notifications are limited to the 10 most recent per person, and invite codes expire after 24 hours.
  • Backups held by our providers are overwritten on their normal schedule.

6a. How to delete your account and data

In the app: open Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account and type DELETE to confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes:

  • your account, profile, push tokens, invite codes, location, public encryption key and recovery backup;
  • everything you shared with your partner: moods, memories and their images, timeline, canvas drawings, notifications, daily-question answers, bucket-list items, your next-meeting date and streak data (this is shared data, so your partner's copy is deleted too, and they are disconnected).

Without the app (for example if you uninstalled it): email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on your Google account with the subject "Delete my Usync account". We will delete the account and its data within 30 days. Backups held by our providers are overwritten on their normal schedule.

7. Security

End-to-end encryption. Your moods, daily-question answers, bucket-list items, next-meeting date and the details of your notifications are encrypted on your phone with a key that only you and your partner hold, before they are sent to us. We, our providers and anyone with access to our database see only unreadable text. Content created before you updated the app, and the other content described above (memories and their images, timeline entries, canvas drawings and location), is not yet end-to-end encrypted; it is protected by encrypted connections and access rules, and we plan to extend the encryption to it. Even with encryption we can still see information about use: who is paired with whom, when and how often things are created, the kind of activity, streaks and counts, your email address and display name from Google, your avatar and relationship type. Encryption cannot protect against someone who has your unlocked phone or whom you let see the screen, a screenshot taken by your partner, or an app update that is itself malicious; compare the security code with your partner in Profile → Security to detect a swapped key. You are responsible for your recovery phrase: if you lose your phone and the phrase, and your partner's phone cannot approve a new key, your encrypted history cannot be recovered by anyone.

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each couple's data is protected by access rules so only its two members can read it, memory images are kept in a private area and shown through short-lived links, and push tokens are not readable by your partner. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
