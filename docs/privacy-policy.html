Usync Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

Usync ("Usync", "the app", "we") is an app for two people to stay emotionally connected: a shared drawing canvas, touch sync, mood sharing, a daily question, a shared bucket list, memories, a timeline, a private chat and couple games. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. Usync is provided by Anuket Kumar.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhat it is and why we need it
Google account detailsWhen you sign in with Google we receive your email address, your Google display name and an account identifier. We use them to create and recognise your account. We never see your Google password.
ProfileYour display name (taken from Google, and editable) and an avatar emoji. Your partner sees these.
PairingThe invite code you generate or enter, and the link between you and your partner. Invite codes expire after 24 hours.
MoodsThe moods you send (including custom text and emoji) and when you sent them. The mood itself is end-to-end encrypted (see section 7); we can see only that a mood was sent, by whom and when.
Canvas and memoriesDrawings you make on the shared canvas, and the memories you save, including their images, titles and dates. Images are stored in a private storage area. The memories you save (title and picture) and the shared drawing are end-to-end encrypted; we cannot read them.
Private chatThe text messages, photos and voice notes you and your partner send each other, who sent them and when. Everything is end-to-end encrypted on your phone before it is sent; we cannot read it. Your chat history is stored on your phone, not on our servers: a message waits on our server, still encrypted, only until your partner's phone has received it, then it is deleted (a message nobody collects is deleted after 14 days). Read receipts and "delete for everyone" requests travel the same way, encrypted, so we do not learn when a message was read. There is no backup of your chat: if you lose or reinstall your phone, its chat history is gone. You can choose a timer so messages vanish after being read; by default they stay on your phones until you delete them.
GamesWhen you play a game together (tic-tac-toe, quiz, would-you-rather, conversation cards, Slap, Pen Fight) we store the state of the current game, such as the board, whose turn it is and the questions, and who started it. In the quiz and would-you-rather games your personal answers are end-to-end encrypted, and your partner's answer is shown only after you have answered. Only your couple's most recent finished game is kept; starting a new game deletes the previous finished ones. Live moves in Slap and Pen Fight are sent in real time and are not kept.
Touch and presenceLive touch events and whether you are online are sent between the two of you in real time. They are not kept after the moment passes.
Location (optional)If you turn on location sharing, your last known location is stored and shown to your partner. It is updated when you open the app, and in the background only if you allow background location and your partner asks for it; you are notified each time. You control this in the app and in your phone's settings. The location itself is end-to-end encrypted; we cannot read it, and can only see that it was updated and when.
NotificationsThe alerts you and your partner send each other. The text of an alert that is sent to your phone's lock screen is a fixed sentence such as "Alex shared a mood 💭"; it never contains what you typed, but it does show the kind of activity (for example a mood, a bucket-list item or a touch) and who did it. The details are stored end-to-end encrypted and shown only inside the app. We keep only the 10 most recent per person. We also store a push token for each of your devices so we can deliver them, and your choices about which alerts are silent.
Daily questionYour answers to the question of the day (up to 500 characters each) and the day you gave them. Answers are end-to-end encrypted; we cannot read them. Your partner's answer is only shown to you after you have answered, and you only ever see your own answer before that.
Bucket listThe items you and your partner add to your shared bucket list (title, optional note, emoji) are end-to-end encrypted; we cannot read them. We can see who added an item, when, and when an item was ticked off and by whom.
Relationship type and next meetingWhether you chose "Together" or "Long-distance" for your couple, and, if you set one, the date you plan to meet next. Both are shared with your partner. The date is end-to-end encrypted; the relationship type is not.
Encryption keysTo protect your data with end-to-end encryption we store your public encryption key (your partner can see it), a recovery backup of your key that is sealed with a phrase only you know (we cannot open it), and your couple's key sealed separately for each of you (we cannot open it). We never receive your private key, your recovery phrase or your couple's key. If you lose your phone and your recovery phrase, nobody, including us, can recover your encrypted history.
StreaksThe days on which both of you did something in the app, used to show your day streak and moment count.
On your device onlyYour theme, notification and other settings, and a cached copy of your recent data and changes waiting to be sent, so the app works offline. This local copy, and memory pictures saved on your phone, are encrypted with a key kept in your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). The picture shown in the home-screen widget, and any picture you choose to save to your gallery or share, are ordinary files. Biometric lock is handled entirely by your phone's operating system; we never receive or store your fingerprint or face data.

We do not use advertising, we do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we do not collect your contacts. The app reads a photo only when you choose one (or take one) to send in the chat or save as a memory, and uses the microphone only while you hold the microphone button to record a voice note; it does not browse your photo library or listen in the background.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To connect you with your partner and run the features you use: canvas, touch sync, moods, the daily question, the bucket list, memories, timeline, private chat, games, streaks and location sharing.
  • To send you push notifications when your partner does something.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to prevent abuse.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Who can see your information

  • Your partner sees your profile, moods, canvas, memories, timeline, chat messages, game activity, streaks, online status and, if you share it, your location. Nobody else in the app can see this. Access is limited to the two members of a couple by database access rules.
  • Service providers that process data for us (below). They may only use it to provide their service to us.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect people's safety.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications to alert you when your partner sends something.
  • Camera and photos only when you choose to take or pick a picture for the chat or a memory. You can use Usync without them.
  • Microphone only while you hold the button to record a chat voice note.
  • Location (precise, approximate, and optionally background) only for location sharing. You can use Usync without it.
  • Biometrics / screen lock to protect the app. The app lock is on by default and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • Internet to talk to our servers.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • Disconnecting from your partner (Settings → Disconnect Couple) permanently deletes everything you shared: moods, memories and their images, timeline, canvas drawings, notifications, locations, daily-question answers, bucket-list items, games, any chat messages still waiting on our servers (and the chat history on your phone), your next-meeting date, your couple's sealed encryption keys and streak data. It cannot be undone.
  • Signing out removes your location from the server and your push token for that device, and clears the app's data on that device.
  • Deleting your account is described in section 6a below.
  • Notifications are limited to the 10 most recent per person, and invite codes expire after 24 hours; expired codes are removed from our servers once a month.
  • Chat: your chat history lives on your phone and stays there until you delete it (or the timer removes it). "Delete for me" removes a message from your phone only; "Delete for everyone" (your own messages) and "Clear for both phones" also remove it from your partner's phone when it next connects. On our servers a message is deleted as soon as your partner's phone has received it, and in any case after 14 days; its photo or voice file is removed with it. Signing out, disconnecting from your partner or deleting your account clears the chat history on that phone.
  • Games: only your couple's latest finished game is kept; starting a new game deletes the previous finished ones and their answers.
  • Backups held by our providers are overwritten on their normal schedule.

6a. How to delete your account and data

In the app: open Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account and type DELETE to confirm. This immediately and permanently deletes:

  • your account, profile, push tokens, invite codes, location, public encryption key and recovery backup;
  • everything you shared with your partner: moods, memories and their images, timeline, canvas drawings, notifications, daily-question answers, bucket-list items, games, any chat messages still waiting on our servers, your next-meeting date and streak data (this is shared data, so your partner's copy is deleted too, and they are disconnected).

Without the app (for example if you uninstalled it): email charlesramanewton@gmail.com from the address on your Google account with the subject "Delete my Usync account". We will delete the account and its data within 30 days. Backups held by our providers are overwritten on their normal schedule.

7. Security

End-to-end encryption. Your moods, daily-question answers, bucket-list items, next-meeting date, memories (title and picture), shared drawing, location, the details of your notifications, chat messages, photos and voice notes, and your personal game answers are encrypted on your phone with a key that only you and your partner hold, before they are sent to us. We, our providers and anyone with access to our database see only unreadable text. Content created before you updated the app, and timeline entries, are not yet end-to-end encrypted; they are protected by encrypted connections and access rules, and we plan to extend the encryption to them. Even with encryption we can still see information about use: who is paired with whom, when and how often things are created, the kind of activity, streaks and counts, your email address and display name from Google, your avatar and relationship type. Encryption cannot protect against someone who has your unlocked phone or whom you let see the screen, a screenshot taken by your partner, or an app update that is itself malicious; compare the security code with your partner in Profile → Security to detect a swapped key. You are responsible for your recovery phrase: if you lose your phone and the phrase, and your partner's phone cannot approve a new key, your encrypted history cannot be recovered by anyone.

Your chat history is kept on your phone in storage encrypted with a key held in your phone's secure storage, and is not uploaded anywhere else. Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each couple's data is protected by access rules so only its two members can read it, memory images are kept in a private area and shown through short-lived links, and push tokens are not readable by your partner. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You can edit your name and avatar, switch location sharing, notifications and the app lock on or off, disconnect from your partner, and sign out at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict how it is used. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. Children

Usync is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, they rely on recognised safeguards for such transfers.
