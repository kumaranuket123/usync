# Feature: Private chat

A WhatsApp-style chat between the two partners: text, photos and voice notes. Everything is end-to-end encrypted on the phone, and a message disappears for both partners a while after the receiver has seen it: the sender chooses 10 seconds, 1 minute, 5 minutes, 1 hour or 1 day (the default). A message nobody opens is deleted after 1 day.

## Screen: Private chat (`/chat`)
Opened from the Home "Chat" pill, the Activity tab card, and a chat notification in the notification list.
- Header: back button, title with "🔒 Encrypted" under it, and a **timer button** (⏱ with the current choice) that opens a sheet with 10 seconds / 1 minute / 5 minutes / 1 hour / 1 day. The timer is **shared by the couple**: either partner can change it, it is stored on the couple (`couples.chat_ttl_seconds`, default 1 day) and both phones show the same value (loaded when the chat opens and kept live through the `couples` row stream). It applies to every new message from either partner, counted from when the receiver sees it; messages already sent keep the timer they were sent with. The last known value is also kept on the phone (`PrefsSource.chatTimerSeconds`) so the chat has one before the server answers. The list shows the newest message at the bottom (`ListView(reverse: true)`).
- **Bubbles**: yours on the right (pink), your partner's on the left (cyan). There is no countdown on messages; every bubble shows the time it was sent (in the phone's own time format) and your own bubbles also show a quiet tick, one when sent and two (in colour) once your partner has seen it. A message this phone cannot open (no couple key yet) shows "Locked until your key arrives".
- **Reply**: drag any bubble (yours or your partner's) to the right; past a short distance the phone ticks and, on release, a quote bar (who wrote it, and the text, "📷 Photo" or "🎤 Voice note") appears above the input with a ✕ to drop it. The next message sent (text, photo or voice) is a reply and shows the quote at the top of its bubble. The quote is a copy of up to 120 characters kept inside the message's sealed content (`ChatReply`, key `r`), so it needs no server change and still shows after the original has vanished. Locked messages cannot be replied to.
- **Delete**: hold any bubble (yours or your partner's, text, photo or voice) and choose "Delete message" in the sheet. It is deleted for both of you, and its photo or voice file with it. It leaves your screen at once; if the server cannot be reached it comes back with "Could not delete the message".
- **Clear chat**: the ⋮ menu in the header has "Clear chat"; after a confirmation every message, photo and voice note is deleted for both of you (cannot be undone). The chat empties at once and everything comes back with a notice if the server cannot be reached.
- **Timer lines**: when the timer changes, a quiet centred line ("⏱ You set messages to vanish 1 minute after seen" / "Your partner set …") appears in the list right after the newest message, on both phones if both have the chat open. It is shown only for the visit (not stored on the server), so it is gone the next time the chat is opened; clearing the chat removes it.
- **Unread badge**: the Chat button on Home shows a small pink number just above it ("9+" past nine) while your partner has sent messages you have not seen. `IChatRepository.watchUnreadCount` counts the partner's rows with no `seen_at` that have not expired, from the same table stream; the partner's delete and clear Broadcasts lower it at once. It goes to zero as the chat marks messages seen.
- **Text**: multi-line field (max 2000 characters); the send button replaces the microphone once there is text.
- **Reveal to start the timer (photos and voice notes)**: a partner's photo is shown blurred with "Tap to reveal" and a partner's voice note waits to be played; the timer starts only when you tap the photo or press play (`ChatMediaRevealed` -> `mark_chat_seen` for that id), and until then it stays. Your own photos are always clear. Text still starts its timer when it is on your screen. The sender's ✓✓ turns blue when the partner has revealed the photo or voice note. An unrevealed photo or voice note is kept for at most 7 days (a safety limit; unrevealed text 1 day).
- **Photo**: every photo takes the same fixed 200 x 200 box in the chat (cropped to fit, so a large picture does not fill the screen); tap it to see the whole picture full screen. The add-photo button offers camera or gallery. The picture is shrunk to at most 1600 px at quality 80 before sending. Tap a photo to view it full screen (pinch to zoom).
- **Voice note**: hold the microphone to record (AAC, mono), release to send, slide left to cancel. Under 0.8 s is discarded, 2 minutes is the limit. Played from memory with a play/pause button and progress bar.
- On Android the screen is protected while the chat is open: screenshots, screen recording and the app-switcher preview are blocked (`SecureScreen`, `FLAG_SECURE` in `MainActivity`). iOS cannot block them.

## State
`ChatBloc` (`ChatOpened`, `ChatVisibilityChanged`, `ChatTimerChanged`, `ChatTextSent`, `ChatImageSent`, `ChatVoiceSent`; private `_MessagesChanged`, `_Tick`). `ChatState`: `loading`, `messages` (oldest first, expired ones already left out), `now`, `userId`, `timer`, `notice` (a one-off message: no key, offline, failed).
- A 1-second ticker drops the messages that have expired (it only redraws when one did); the server deletes rows, but Realtime does not report deletes, so the phone drops expired messages itself.
- A partner's TEXT message is reported as seen (`mark_chat_seen`) only while the chat is in front and the app is resumed, once per message; a failure is retried on the next change. A photo or voice note is reported only when it is revealed.
- While the chat is in front the phone announces `screen: chat` on Presence (and takes it back when it leaves, goes to the background or is closed).

## Data
- Migrations `supabase/migrations/couple_chat.sql` (apply after `couple_games_slap.sql`), then `couple_chat_timer.sql` (a `ttl_seconds` per message, allowed values 10/60/300/3600/86400, and a 1-day life for unseen messages), then `couple_chat_delete.sql` (the `delete_chat_message` RPC), then `couple_chat_clear.sql` (the `clear_chat` RPC), then `couple_chat_shared_timer.sql` (the couple's `chat_ttl_seconds`, the `set_chat_timer` RPC, and `send_chat_message` now stamps each message with the couple's timer; the `p_ttl` argument is still accepted but no longer decides), then `couple_chat_reveal.sql` (an unrevealed photo or voice note lives 7 days instead of 1), then `couple_chat_hardening.sql` (expired rows are no longer readable; clients can read, add and replace chat files but not delete them). Edge Function `supabase/functions/purge-chat/`.
- `chat_messages`: `id` (chosen by the sender), `couple_id`, `sender_id`, `kind` (`text` | `image` | `voice`), `enc`, `media_path`, `ttl_seconds`, `created_at`, `seen_at`, `expires_at`. Clients can only select (RLS `is_active_couple_member`); writes go through the RPCs `send_chat_message` (checks the member, the sender and that the media path is exactly `<couple>/<id>.enc`; a retry of the same id changes nothing) and `mark_chat_seen` (only the partner of the sender: sets `seen_at = now()` and `expires_at = now() + ttl_seconds`). `send_chat_message` stamps each message with the couple's timer (`couples.chat_ttl_seconds`) when it reaches the server; the `p_ttl` argument the app still sends is checked against the five choices but does not decide.
- Sealing: `enc` is `ContentCipher.sealJson` of `{t: text, d: durationMs}` bound to couple, table `chat_messages` and the message id; the photo or voice bytes are `sealBytes` (field `media`) in the private `chat` bucket at `<couple_id>/<message id>.enc`. The server sees only ciphertext, sender, kind, sizes and timestamps (including when it was seen).
- Deleting: either partner can delete any message of their couple through `delete_chat_message(p_id)` (SECURITY DEFINER, active-member check; unknown or already deleted ids do nothing). Clients still have no delete policy. The row delete fires the same `AFTER DELETE` trigger as expiry, so the file is queued and removed by `purge-chat` within a minute. Because Realtime does not report deletes, the deleting phone also sends a `chat_delete` Broadcast (`{ids, from}`) on the couple channel; the partner's phone drops those ids at once (`ChatRepository.watchDeleted`). A phone that was offline never sees the message again when it next loads the chat, since the row is gone.
- Clearing: `clear_chat(p_couple)` (SECURITY DEFINER, active-member check) deletes all of the couple's messages; the trigger queues every file for `purge-chat`. The clearing phone also sends a `chat_clear` Broadcast so the partner's phone empties at once; messages sent afterwards show as usual.
- Expiry: the cron job `purge-chat` runs `purge_chat()` every minute: it deletes expired rows (an `AFTER DELETE` trigger queues each `media_path` in `chat_media_trash`), then calls the `purge-chat` Edge Function (same Vault secret as `send-push`) which removes the queued files from Storage. Disconnecting the couple (`couples.disconnected_at` set, which account deletion also does) clears the couple's chat through a trigger.
- Decrypted photos and voice notes are kept in memory only (12 most recent), never written to the phone. The recorder's temporary file is deleted as soon as it has been read.
- Realtime: `chat_messages` is in the `supabase_realtime` publication with full replica identity, so a seen/expiry change reaches the sender's phone.
- Push: `NotifType.chat`, own "Chat" alert-sound group. The text is fixed ("New message 💬": no name, no content). Sent only when the partner is not on the chat screen (Presence) and at most once a minute.
- Sending a message counts toward the day streak (`record_activity`).

## Edge cases
- **No couple key yet**: sending is refused with "Finish securing your connection first: Profile → Security", nothing is queued or sent; received messages show as locked.
- **Offline**: sends go through the `OutboxService` (`chat_message`, with the message id and the media bytes, encrypted at rest on the phone); they are sent in order when the connection is back. The message appears in the chat once it reaches the server.
- **Microphone / camera / photos denied**: the person is told to allow the microphone, or the photo cannot be opened.
- **Partner absent**: the message waits (1 day); its timer starts when they open it.
- **Short timers**: with 10 seconds the phones hide the message at once, but the server deletes it and its file with the next cron run (within a minute); the partner can no longer read the row after it has expired.
- **Not a screenshot-proof chat**: the partner can still photograph the screen or save a photo, and iOS screenshots are not blocked.
