# Feature: Private chat

A WhatsApp / Signal style chat between the two partners: text, photos and voice notes. Everything is end-to-end encrypted on the phone. **The history lives on the phones**: the server is only a mailbox that holds a message until the partner's phone has saved it and said so, then deletes it. Messages stay on the phones until someone deletes them, unless the couple turns on the optional vanish timer. There is no backup: a lost, wiped or reinstalled phone loses its history (new messages still arrive).

## Screen: Private chat (`/chat`)
Opened from the Home "Chat" pill, the Activity tab card, and a chat notification in the notification list.
- Header: back button, title with "🔒 Encrypted" under it, a **timer button** (⏱ with the current choice) that opens a sheet with Off / 10 seconds / 1 minute / 5 minutes / 1 hour / 1 day, and a ⋮ menu. The timer is **shared by the couple** (stored on `couples.chat_ttl_seconds`, default **0 = Off**; either partner can change it, both phones show the same value, loaded when the chat opens and kept live through the `couples` row stream). It applies to every new message from either partner; messages already sent keep the timer they were sent with. The last known value is also kept on the phone (`PrefsSource.chatTimerSeconds`). The list shows the newest message at the bottom (`ListView(reverse: true)`).
- **Opens instantly and pages**: the chat reads only the phone's own history. It shows the newest 30 messages; scrolling near the top loads 30 more (`ChatOlderRequested` -> `IChatRepository.loadOlder`). A day with 1000 messages opens as fast as one with 10.
- **Bubbles**: yours on the right (pink), your partner's on the left (cyan). Every bubble shows the time it was sent, and your own bubbles show the delivery state: a clock while the message waits for a connection, one tick when the server has it, two grey ticks when your partner's phone has it (`delivered`), two blue ticks when your partner has read it (`read`).
- **Day labels**: a small centred pill above the first message of each day says "Today", "Yesterday" or the date ("12 Nov", with the year when it is not this year), by the phone's local day (`dayLabel`). Timer lines get one too when they start a day.
- **Reply**: drag any bubble to the right; past a short distance the phone ticks and, on release, a quote bar (who wrote it, and the text, "📷 Photo" or "🎤 Voice note") appears above the input with a ✕ to drop it. The next message sent (text, photo or voice) is a reply and shows the quote at the top of its bubble. The quote is a copy of up to 120 characters kept inside the message's sealed content (`ChatReply`, key `r`), so it still shows after the original is gone. **Tap the quote** in a bubble to jump to the message it answers: the list scrolls there (loading older pages first if it is further back) and the bubble flashes for a moment; if the original was deleted or has vanished, a notice says "The original message is no longer available".
- **Delete**: hold a bubble. "Delete for me" removes it from this phone only (any message). On your own messages there is also "Delete for everyone": it is removed from your phone, from the mailbox if your partner has not collected it yet (`delete_chat_message`), and a sealed `delete` control tells your partner's phone to remove it (also later, if that phone is offline). You can take back only what you wrote; your partner's phone ignores a delete for a message that is not yours.
- **Clear chat**: the ⋮ menu has "Clear chat" with "This phone" (local only) or "Both phones" (also `clear_chat` on the server and a `clear` control; your partner's phone removes everything sent at or before the moment of the clear).
- **Timer lines**: when the timer changes, a quiet centred line ("⏱ You set messages to vanish 1 minute after read" / "Your partner turned vanishing messages off") appears after the newest message, on both phones if both have the chat open. It is shown only for the visit and is not stored.
- **Unread badge**: the Chat button on Home shows a small pink number ("9+" past nine) for partner messages on this phone that you have not read, counted from the local history (`IChatRepository.watchUnreadCount`). Home starts collecting messages as soon as it loads, so the badge is right without opening the chat.
- **Text**: multi-line field (max 2000 characters); the send button replaces the microphone once there is text.
- **Reveal (photos and voice notes)**: a partner's photo is shown blurred with "Tap to reveal" and a voice note waits to be played; that counts as reading it (`ChatMediaRevealed` -> `markSeen`): the blue ticks go to the sender and, with a timer on, the countdown starts. Your own photos are always clear. **With the timer Off (the default) nothing is hidden**: a partner's photo shows clearly and counts as read when it is on your screen (like a text); a voice note still counts as read when played. The blur applies only to messages sent while a timer is on (`ttlSeconds > 0`). A text counts as read when it is on your screen with the chat in front.
- **Photo**: a fixed 200 x 200 box in the chat (cropped to fit, decoded at 400 px wide); tap to see the whole picture full screen (pinch to zoom). The add-photo button offers camera or gallery. The picture is shrunk to at most 1600 px at quality 80 before sending.
- **Voice note**: hold the microphone to record (AAC, mono), release to send, slide left to cancel. Under 0.8 s is discarded, 2 minutes is the limit. Played from memory with a play/pause button and progress bar.
- On Android the screen is protected while the chat is open: screenshots, screen recording and the app-switcher preview are blocked (`SecureScreen`, `FLAG_SECURE` in `MainActivity`). iOS cannot block them.

## How a message travels
1. **Send**: the message is written to the phone's history at once (state *pending*) and sealed and queued in the `OutboxService` (`chat_message`), so it appears immediately, also offline. `send_chat_message` stores the sealed row in the mailbox and returns the server's time; the phone then marks it sent and orders it by that time, like the partner's.
2. **Collect** (`ChatRepository.startInbox`, started by Home and the chat; a Realtime stream of the couple's mailbox rows): for each row from the partner the phone opens it with the couple key, downloads and opens the photo or voice file, **saves it to its history, and only then** calls `ack_chat_messages`, which deletes the row (a trigger queues the sealed file for `purge-chat`). A crash in between only delivers it again; a saved id is skipped. A row that cannot be opened yet (no couple key, file missing) stays on the server and is tried again.
3. **Receipts and deletes** are *control* envelopes (`kind = 'control'`, sealed like a message, sent through the outbox as `chat_control`, no push): `{c: 'receipt', k: 'delivered' | 'read', ids}`, `{c: 'delete', ids}`, `{c: 'clear'}`. The partner's phone applies them and acks them like messages, so they also reach a phone that was offline.
4. **Undelivered limit**: a row nobody collects is deleted after 14 days (`expires_at`, by `purge_chat()` every minute).
5. **Optional timer** (Off by default): a message carries the couple's timer when it is sent. It starts when the message is read: on the reader's phone at once, on the sender's phone when the `read` receipt arrives. Each phone deletes it from its own history (`purgeExpired`, run when the chat opens and whenever the 1-second ticker sees one expire). The server takes no part.

## Local history
`ChatLocalSource` (`lib/data/datasources/local/chat_local_source.dart`): three Hive boxes encrypted with the phone's local key (`DeviceKey`, opened in `injection.dart` with `openProtectedBox` / `openProtectedLazyBox`): `chat_messages_enc` (lazy, one map per message keyed by its send time as a zero-padded string, so a page is a slice of the sorted keys), `chat_index_enc` (id -> position, unread, expiry; small enough to scan; also the owner `userId:coupleId`) and `chat_media_enc` (lazy, the decrypted photo / voice bytes). `ChatMessageModel` converts a message to and from its map.
- The history belongs to one account and couple: `startInbox` claims it, and another account's history is thrown away. It is also wiped at sign-out, on account deletion and when the couple is disconnected.
- If the phone's local key is lost (a restore, for example) the boxes cannot be opened and start empty. If they disagree with each other (one recovered, another not) all three are reset. When the Keystore / Keychain is not usable at all the history is kept in memory only for that run, so it is lost on restart (the server no longer has a copy).
- Collected ids are remembered for 15 days (tombstones in the index box), also after a message is deleted, so a stale copy still on the server, a retried collect or a "delete for everyone" that arrives before its message cannot bring it back. Writes are serialised. `IChatRepository.reset()` (sign-out, disconnect) stops collecting and wipes everything.
- **First run after the update**: the user's own messages the old server still holds are imported once, and old partner rows that were already read keep the timer the old server set.

## State
`ChatBloc` (`ChatOpened`, `ChatVisibilityChanged`, `ChatTimerChanged`, `ChatTextSent`, `ChatImageSent`, `ChatVoiceSent`, `ChatMessageDeleted(forEveryone)`, `ChatCleared(forEveryone)`, `ChatOlderRequested`, `ChatMediaRevealed`, `ChatReplyStarted/Cancelled`; private `_MessagesChanged`, `_Tick`, `_TimerFromServer`). `ChatState`: `loading`, `messages` (oldest first, expired ones left out), `now`, `userId`, `timer`, `notice`, `notices`, `replyingTo`, `hasOlder`.
- The messages stream is the phone's own history, so deletes, clears and receipts from the partner simply change it; the bloc keeps no list of deleted ids.
- A partner's TEXT message is reported read (`markSeen`: local state, then a sealed `read` receipt) only while the chat is in front and the app is resumed, once per message. A photo or voice note is reported only when revealed.
- While the chat is in front the phone announces `screen: chat` on Presence (and takes it back when it leaves, goes to the background or is closed).

## Data
- Migration `supabase/migrations/chat_mailbox.sql` (apply after `couple_chat_shared_timer.sql`; **old app builds do not work with it**, raise the minimum build with `get_app_config` first or together): the `control` kind, timer value `0`, `couples.chat_ttl_seconds` default 0 (couples still on the old 1-day default move to Off), `set_chat_timer` accepting 0, `send_chat_message` returning the server time and giving every row a 14-day mailbox life, the new `ack_chat_messages(p_ids)` (only the partner of the sender, active couple, at most 1000 ids), `chat_tombstones` (ids of rows already gone, kept 15 days, so a retried send of a delivered / deleted message or control is not put back), `delete_chat_message` now only for the sender (taking back an undelivered message), `clear_chat` leaving control envelopes alone, control rows not counting for the streak, and the removal of `mark_chat_seen`. Earlier chat migrations: `couple_chat.sql`, `couple_chat_timer.sql`, `couple_chat_delete.sql`, `couple_chat_clear.sql`, `couple_chat_shared_timer.sql`, `couple_chat_reveal.sql`, `couple_chat_hardening.sql`. Edge Function `supabase/functions/purge-chat/`.
- `chat_messages` (the mailbox): `id` (chosen by the sender), `couple_id`, `sender_id`, `kind` (`text` | `image` | `voice` | `control`), `enc`, `media_path`, `ttl_seconds`, `created_at`, `expires_at`. Clients can only select; writes go through `send_chat_message` (member check, sender, media path exactly `<couple>/<id>.enc`, a retry of the same id changes nothing), `ack_chat_messages`, `delete_chat_message` (an undelivered message, either partner) and `clear_chat`.
- Sealing: `enc` is `ContentCipher.sealJson` bound to couple, table `chat_messages` and the row id: `{t, d, r}` for a message (text, voice length, reply quote) or the control map; photo / voice bytes are `sealBytes` (field `media`) in the private `chat` bucket at `<couple_id>/<message id>.enc`. The server sees only ciphertext, sender, kind, sizes and send time, and only until the partner collects it. It no longer learns when a message was read.
- Files: removing a row (ack, delete, clear, 14-day expiry) fires the `AFTER DELETE` trigger that queues its `media_path` in `chat_media_trash`; the cron job `purge-chat` (every minute) calls the Edge Function that removes the queued files. Disconnecting the couple clears the mailbox through a trigger and the phones clear their history.
- Realtime: `chat_messages` is in the `supabase_realtime` publication; the phones' mailbox stream is what makes a message arrive.
- Push: `NotifType.chat`, own "Chat" alert-sound group. The text is fixed ("New message 💬"). Sent only when the partner is not on the chat screen (Presence) and at most once a minute; control envelopes send none.
- Sending a message counts toward the day streak (`record_activity`).

## Edge cases
- **No couple key yet**: sending is refused with "Finish securing your connection first: Profile → Security"; received messages stay in the mailbox until the key arrives, then are collected.
- **Offline**: sends and receipts wait in the outbox and go in order when the connection is back; the bubble shows a clock until then.
- **Partner offline**: the message waits in the mailbox (14 days); "Delete for everyone" and "Clear for both" reach them when they next collect.
- **Microphone / camera / photos denied**: the person is told to allow the microphone, or the photo cannot be opened.
- **New phone or reinstall**: history is gone (no backup); messages still in the mailbox are delivered.
- **Delete for everyone is best effort**: a partner who already read or saved the message elsewhere, or an old app build, may keep it.
- **Not a screenshot-proof chat**: the partner can still photograph the screen or save a photo, and iOS screenshots are not blocked.
